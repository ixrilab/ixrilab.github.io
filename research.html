Augmented Perception for Embodied Intelligence

XR for Physical AI

Most Physical AI research asks how to give robots better sensors and bigger models. We ask a complementary question: what if XR became the infrastructure that lets robots perceive, remember, and coordinate beyond what any single agent can do alone? XR is no longer just for human eyes — it is the substrate that augments embodied intelligence.

Beyond the Robot's Eye

Today's Physical AI sees only what its sensors capture in this instant — blind to occluded objects, off-camera state, future intent, and the invisible infrastructure of a real building. We treat XR as a live world model that the robot can query: the gate that will lock at five, the colleague approaching from around the corner, the affordance of a door it has never opened. Perception becomes a layered service, not the agent's sole responsibility.

Persistent Spatial Memory

Robots forget too quickly. Building on our XRMemory foundations, we anchor episodic memory to space — "this trolley was here yesterday at 09:00", "the supplies arrived in Room 201 last Tuesday" — so Physical AI can carry context across sessions, shifts, and days, the way humans do when returning to a familiar place.

Long-horizon & Continuous Management

Real-world work is rarely a single episode. Site stewardship, equipment monitoring, ward rounds — these tasks span hours to weeks, demand detection of subtle deviation from a learned baseline, and require stable spatial context for multi-step planning. The XR layer is what makes long-horizon Physical AI tractable rather than just demo-friendly.

Shared World, Human-in-the-Loop

One world model, many readers. Multiple Physical AI agents subscribe to the same XR substrate to coordinate without collisions or duplicate work, while human supervisors annotate, correct, and audit the model through AR. The result is a two-way spatial language between people and machines — closer to teamwork than to remote control.

Our testbed is La Trobe's Bundoora campus, where real robots, IoT sensors, and AR clients exercise the XR substrate on day-to-day operations. NVIDIA Omniverse provides the simulation backend for sim-to-real training; Cisco's network infrastructure carries the substrate.

Spatial Experience Capture & Replay

XRMemory

Record the Essence, Generate the Context

We capture what truly matters — hand-object interactions, spatial relationships, semantic context — using our Spatial Event Record Structure (SERS), a scene graph standard with A/M/D/R frame hierarchy inspired by video coding. The essence at a fraction of the cost.

Cross-Environment Replay

Record a work session in Seoul, replay it in Melbourne — not as flat video, but as a spatial experience you can step into. Anchor-relative UV parameterization adapts trajectories to new surfaces while preserving physical plausibility.

Bridging Distance, Restoring the Past

Remote collaboration that transcends distance, and memory restoration that lets you revisit past experiences — a workshop, a family gathering — as immersive, walkable moments.

Intelligent Compression with KeyScene

Our KeyScene Selection Method identifies critical interaction moments and reconstructs smooth trajectories via Hermite interpolation. Up to 45× compression with sub-centimetre accuracy.

Digital Self & Autonomous Agency

Ultimate Selfie

Your Digital Self in the Digital World

Not just an avatar or a chatbot — a faithful replica of your cognition, work ethic, and identity that can represent you in the digital world.

Interacting across Realities through XR

Meet your digital self in space. Through AR it appears in your physical environment; in VR you step into its world. XR becomes the bridge between you and your twin.

Autonomous Work & Daily Sync

While you rest, your Selfie continues working — drafting, reviewing, researching in your style. Every day you synchronise, course-correct, and stay aligned as one.

Self-Modelling at Scale

We model the multi-dimensional nature of a person — cognitive patterns, expertise, communication style — from heterogeneous data, creating a living representation that grows more faithful over time.
