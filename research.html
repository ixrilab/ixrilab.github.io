Digital Self & Autonomous Agency

Ultimate Selfie

Your Digital Self in the Digital World

Ultimate Selfie is the pursuit of creating a complete digital twin of yourself — not just an avatar or a chatbot, but a faithful replica of your cognition, work ethic, and identity. By learning how you think, how you make decisions, how you've worked throughout your career, and how you relate to the people around you, your Ultimate Selfie becomes a true digital self that can represent you in the digital world.

Interacting across Realities through XR

Your Ultimate Selfie doesn't live behind a screen — you meet it in space. Through AR, your digital self appears in your physical environment as a spatial presence you can converse with, review work alongside, and collaborate with face-to-face. In VR, you step into your Selfie's world to inspect what it has been doing, walk through its decisions, and experience its context firsthand. XR becomes the bridge that lets you and your digital twin move fluidly between the physical and digital worlds.

Autonomous Work & Daily Sync

While you sleep, rest, or focus on other things, your Ultimate Selfie continues working on your behalf — drafting emails, reviewing documents, conducting research, and managing tasks in your style. Every day, you and your Selfie synchronise: it reports what it accomplished, you course-correct and share new context, and the two of you stay aligned as one coherent identity operating across both worlds.

Self-Modelling at Scale

Building an Ultimate Selfie requires capturing the multi-dimensional nature of a person: cognitive patterns, domain expertise, communication style, interpersonal dynamics, and evolving preferences. We research how to model these facets from heterogeneous data — work logs, conversations, spatial behaviour captured through XR, and social graphs — creating a living, learning representation that grows more faithful over time.

Spatial Experience Capture & Replay

XRMemory

Record the Essence, Generate the Context

XRMemory is a research programme for capturing, structuring, and replaying spatial experiences through XR and AI. We record what truly matters in an experience — hand-object interactions, spatial relationships, and semantic context — using our Spatial Event Record Structure (SERS), a scene graph standard built on a novel A/M/D/R frame hierarchy inspired by video coding principles. Rather than storing raw volumetric data, SERS captures the essence of an experience at a fraction of the cost.

Cross-Environment Replay

Imagine recording a collaborative work session in Seoul and replaying it in Melbourne — not as a flat video, but as a living spatial experience you can step into and interact with. Through anchor-relative UV parameterization, XRMemory maps recorded interactions onto new physical surfaces, adapting trajectories to different desk sizes, room layouts, and spatial configurations while preserving the physical plausibility of every gesture and movement.

Bridging Distance, Restoring the Past

XRMemory opens two powerful possibilities. First, remote collaboration that transcends distance: a colleague in another country can continue your work exactly where you left off, stepping into a spatial recording of your progress and picking up seamlessly. Second, memory restoration: past experiences — a workshop, a family gathering, a lab session — can be reconstructed and revisited as immersive, walkable moments.

Intelligent Compression with KeyScene

Our KeyScene Selection Method (KSM) uses physics-aware detection to identify the critical moments of an interaction — the instant a hand grasps an object, the moment it is set down — and reconstructs smooth trajectories between them using velocity-enriched Hermite interpolation. The result: up to 45× compression with sub-centimetre accuracy, making spatial memories lightweight enough to stream, share, and store at scale.

Campus-Scale Embodied Intelligence

Digital Twin &
Physical AI

Physical AI @ Bundoora Campus

In partnership with Cisco and NVIDIA, we are building a high-fidelity digital twin of the La Trobe University Bundoora campus inside NVIDIA Omniverse. Leveraging world models and Physical AI, we train embodied agents that perceive and interact with the real world through simulation-to-reality transfer.

Smart City Realisation with AR & Robots

By visualising invisible campus data — IoT sensors, building services, energy flows — through AR overlays and autonomous robots, we transform the campus into a living smart-city testbed. The result is an integrated ecosystem where digital twins, AR interfaces, and robotic agents work together to augment how people navigate and experience physical spaces.
